Warm-Up

🔥 Daily Routine Vocabulary (3 minutes)

Do you remember the Baamboozle game? Say these routines out loud in English:

  • wake up / get up / make your bed
  • brush teeth / wash your face / take a shower
  • have breakfast / have lunch / have dinner
  • do homework / come home / go to bed

Now put them in the order YOU do them every day!

Presentation

📌 Present Simple – How to Form It

SubjectRuleExample
I / You / We / Theybase verbI wake up at 7.
He / She / Itverb + -s or -esHe wakes up at 8.
I / You / We / They (negative)don't + base verbI don't go to bed early.
He / She / It (negative)doesn't + base verbShe doesn't eat breakfast.

📌 Spelling: He/She/It +s

RuleVerbHe/She/It
Most verbs: add -splayplays
Verbs ending -s/-sh/-ch/-x/-o: add -eswatch, gowatches, goes
Consonant + y: change y → i + esstudystudies
Irregularhavehas
✅ I wake up at 6:30 every morning.
✅ He plays football after school.
✅ She doesn't watch TV in the morning.
✅ They don't do homework on Sundays.
✅ My dog goes crazy when I come home! 🐶
💡 Time expressions: every day, always, usually, often, sometimes, never, on Mondays, in the morning, at night.

Describe what you do every day AND what your friend or brother/sister does. Use at least 5 sentences.

  • I wake up at… / He wakes up at…
  • I eat… / She eats…

📖 Fatih's Football Team

Fatih trains with his football team every Tuesday and Thursday. Today is Thursday, so right now he is standing on the pitch, waiting for training to start. His coach usually arrives at five o'clock, but today the coach is running late because of traffic. Fatih's best friend, Ege, is stretching near the goal. Ege always warms up before training — he says it stops injuries. Two other boys are kicking a ball to each other while they wait. Fatih doesn't like waiting; he prefers to be active. Next week, the team is playing a match against another school, so training this week is extra important.

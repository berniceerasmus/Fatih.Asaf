Presentation

📌 Articles: A / An / The

ArticleWhen to useExample
afirst mention / consonant soundI have a dog.
anfirst mention / vowel sound (a e i o u)He has an eagle.
thespecific / already knownThe dog is big.
no articlegeneral plural / uncountableI like cats. I drink water.

📌 Plural Rules

RuleSingularPlural
Most nouns: add -sdog, game, birddogs, games, birds
Ends in -s / -sh / -ch / -x: add -esmatch, bus, foxmatches, buses, foxes
Ends in consonant + y: → iescity, countrycities, countries
Ends in -f / -fe: → veswolf, leafwolves, leaves
Irregularman, woman, child, mouse, footmen, women, children, mice, feet
✅ I saw an eagle in the sky yesterday.
✅ I want a new game for my birthday.
✅ The match starts at 5. — (we both know which match)
✅ I love dogs and wolves. — (no article for general)
✅ There are foxes and deer in the forest.
💡 Trick: Listen to the sound, not the letter! An hour (sounds like "ow"), a university (sounds like "you").

📌 Present Continuous vs Present Simple

Present SimplePresent Continuous
Routines, habits, factsActions happening now, or around now
always, usually, every daynow, right now, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen!
I play football every Saturday.I am playing football right now.
He watches TV every evening.He is watching TV at the moment.
✅ Look! The cat is climbing the tree!
✅ I usually play Minecraft, but right now I am doing homework.
✅ My dad works every day, but today he is resting — he's sick.
✅ Are you listening to music? — No, I'm not.
💡 Spelling tip: run → running (double the last letter), make → making (drop the -e), play → playing (just add -ing).

📖 A Busy Saturday

It's Saturday afternoon at the Asaf house. Right now, Fatih is playing Minecraft with his cousin online. He usually plays football on Saturdays, but today it's raining, so he's staying inside. His mum is cooking lunch in the kitchen — she always makes pasta on Saturdays. His dad is washing the car outside, even though it's raining a little! Fatih's dog, Rex, is sleeping on the sofa; Rex sleeps almost all day, every day. Later, Fatih is going to visit his grandmother, because he visits her every weekend. Right now, though, everyone in the house is busy doing something different.

Write True, False, or Not Given.

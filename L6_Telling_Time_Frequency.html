💡 The key rule — PAST or TO?

Minutes 1–30 → use PAST and say the current hour

Minutes 31–59 → use TO and say the NEXT hour

Think of it like this: are you going toward the next hour, or just past this one?

⏰ Also useful: a.m. = morning  |  p.m. = afternoon / evening

💡 Word order — VERY important!

Frequency adverbs go BEFORE the main verb:

✅ I always eat breakfast.    ✅ They usually play football.

Frequency adverbs go AFTER the verb to be:

✅ She is always happy.    ✅ He is never late.

❌ She always is happy.    ❌ I eat always breakfast.

✅ School starts at half past eight. I always arrive on time.
✅ The match is at quarter to five. We never miss it!
✅ He sometimes plays Minecraft until quarter to midnight! 🎮
✅ My dad usually comes home from work at twenty past six.
✅ I rarely eat fast food — maybe once a month.

📖 Read carefully

My name is Kerem. I usually wake up at quarter to seven. I always have breakfast at seven o'clock — I never skip it! School starts at half past eight. I usually arrive at twenty past eight so I am never late.

After school, I often play football with my friends until quarter to five. I sometimes go to a café with my mum at five o'clock. In the evening, I rarely watch TV — I usually play video games! I always go to bed at ten o'clock. I never stay up past midnight.

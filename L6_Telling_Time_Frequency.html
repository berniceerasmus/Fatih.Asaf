📌 Frequency Adverbs – How often?

AdverbMeaningExample
always100%I always brush my teeth.
usually~80%He usually wakes up at 7.
often~60%We often play football.
sometimes~40%She sometimes watches films.
rarely~20%I rarely eat fast food.
never0%He never misses practice.
✅ School starts at half past eight.
✅ The match is at quarter to five.
✅ I always do my homework after dinner.
✅ He sometimes plays Minecraft until midnight! 🎮
✅ We never miss a Galatasaray match.
💡 Word order: Frequency adverbs go before the main verb but after the verb to be.
✅ I always eat breakfast.    ✅ She is always happy.

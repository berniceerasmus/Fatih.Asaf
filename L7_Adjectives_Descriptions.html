Warm-Up

🔥 Describe It! (3 minutes)

Your teacher shows you pictures. Describe each one in one sentence using an adjective. Can you use different adjectives each time?

  • A lion → "It's a big / powerful / wild animal."
  • A football stadium → "It's a huge / amazing place."
  • Your favourite game → "It's exciting / difficult / fun."

Presentation

📌 Adjective Position

PositionPatternExample
Before nounadjective + nouna fast cheetah
After verb to besubject + be + adjectiveThe cheetah is fast.

📌 Key Adjective Categories

CategoryAdjectives
Sizebig, small, huge, tiny, tall, short
Opinionamazing, boring, exciting, funny, scary, cool, terrible
Appearancebeautiful, ugly, dark, bright, colourful, clean, dirty
Personalityfriendly, shy, brave, lazy, clever, kind, loud
Speed / Strengthfast, slow, strong, weak, powerful
Age / Temperatureold, new, young, hot, cold, warm

✍️ My Hero!

Choose your favourite footballer, game character, or movie hero. Write 7 sentences describing them. Use at least 6 different adjectives.

🗣️ Describe Without Saying the Name!

Think of a famous person, animal, or video game character. Describe them to your teacher using adjectives — but don't say their name! Your teacher guesses.

  • Use at least 4 adjectives
  • Use both positions: before noun AND after verb to be

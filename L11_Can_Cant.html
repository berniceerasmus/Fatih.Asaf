Presentation

📌 Can / Can't – Form

TypeStructureExample
PositiveSubject + can + base verbI can swim.
NegativeSubject + can't + base verbHe can't cook.
QuestionCan + subject + base verb?Can you play chess?
Short answerYes, I can. / No, I can't.Can you dance? — Yes, I can.

📌 Three Uses of Can

UseExample
AbilityI can run fast.
PermissionCan I go to the toilet, please?
Polite requestCan you help me, please?
✅ I can ride a bike, but I can't drive a car.
✅ Can you speak French? — No, I can't, but I can speak Turkish.
✅ Can I borrow your pencil, please? — Yes, of course.
✅ Fish can swim, but they can't walk.
💡 Remember: Can never changes — no -s, even for he/she/it! ✅ She can sing. ❌ She cans sing.

📖 Amazing Animal Abilities

Animals can do incredible things! A cheetah can run at more than 100 kilometres per hour, but it can't run that fast for a long time. An octopus can change colour to hide from danger, and it can also squeeze through tiny spaces. Elephants can't jump — they are the only mammal that can't do it — but they can swim very well using their trunk like a snorkel. Bats can't see very well, but they can "see" using sound instead. Kangaroos can't walk backwards, but they can jump over three metres high! Humans can't do most of these things, but we can learn about them and protect these amazing animals.

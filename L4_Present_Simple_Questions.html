Warm-Up

🔥 Quick Questions (3 minutes)

Your teacher will ask you these questions. Answer in full sentences!

  • Do you play football?
  • Do you like animals?
  • Does your mum cook every day?
  • Does your best friend like video games?

Answer: "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't." / full sentence!

Presentation

📌 How to Make Questions

SubjectHelper verbExample QuestionShort Answer
I / You / We / TheyDoDo you play football?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
He / She / ItDoesDoes he play football?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

📌 Wh- Questions

Wh- wordStructureExample
WhatWhat do/does + subject + verb?What do you eat for breakfast?
WhenWhen do/does + subject + verb?When does he wake up?
WhereWhere do/does + subject + go?Where do they play?
How oftenHow often do/does + subject + verb?How often do you train?
✅ Do you like action movies? — Yes, I do!
✅ Does your friend play Minecraft? — No, he doesn't.
✅ What do you do after school? — I play football.
✅ When does the match start? — It starts at 5 o'clock.
✅ How often do you train? — Three times a week.
💡 Important: When you use does, the main verb goes back to the base form — no -s!
❌ Does he plays?    ✅ Does he play?

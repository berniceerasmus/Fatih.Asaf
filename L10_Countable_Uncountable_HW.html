📖 Picnic Day

Fatih's family is planning a picnic in the park. His mum is packing some sandwiches, a little cheese, and some fruit. She doesn't have any lemonade, so his dad is buying some on the way. There is a lot of sunshine today, so they need some sun cream too. Fatih wants to bring his football and his bike, but there isn't much space in the car. His little sister wants some biscuits, but there aren't any left in the cupboard — they finished them yesterday. Luckily, there is still some chocolate cake from the weekend, so everyone is happy!

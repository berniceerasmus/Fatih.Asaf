Homework Exercises

Write each time fully in English. Remember the past/to rule!

Convert the words into a digital time (e.g. 3:15).

Put the words in the correct order. Pay careful attention to where the adverb goes!

1. I / play / always / football / after / school

2. is / she / late / never

3. sometimes / we / movies / watch / on Saturday

4. usually / my dad / comes home / at / seven o'clock

5. often / are / they / happy / in the morning

6. eats / she / rarely / fast food / but / loves / pizza / she

Complete each sentence with the correct time expression OR frequency adverb.

Each sentence has exactly one mistake. Write the corrected sentence.

1. It's half to five. ❌ (4:30)

2. He plays always football on Sundays. ❌

3. It's twenty past to three. ❌ (2:40)

4. My sister never is on time. ❌

5. It's five past to seven. ❌ (6:55)

6. I usually am hungry after training. ❌

📖 Read carefully

Ali is 13 years old and has a very busy week. He always wakes up at quarter past six from Monday to Friday. He usually has breakfast at half past six and leaves home at twenty past seven.

School starts at eight o'clock and finishes at quarter past three. After school, Ali often goes to football training — it starts at five o'clock and finishes at quarter to seven. He rarely misses it.

In the evenings, he usually does his homework between seven and half past eight. He sometimes plays video games after that — but only until ten o'clock. He never stays up past half past ten.

Answer these questions using information from the text.

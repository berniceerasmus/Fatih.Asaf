📎 Quick Reminder Before You Start

The Rule in 4 Lines

SituationArticleExample
First mention, singular, countablea / anI saw a wolf.
Specific / both know which one / uniquetheThe wolf was huge.
General (all of something) / uncountable–I love – music.
Institutions used for purpose (school, work, bed)–She goes to – school.

Homework Exercises

Write C or U for each word.
Write the or – (a dash if no article needed).
Write a, an, the, or –.
Each sentence has one mistake. Write the corrected version.

1. I don't have a homework tonight. ❌

2. The music is my favourite thing. ❌ (general)

3. She gave me an advice. ❌

4. The cheetahs are faster than lions. ❌ (general fact)

5. I come home from the work at 6. ❌

6. Can I have a water, please? ❌

Look at the bold word and decide: V (verb) or N (noun)?

✍️ My Favourite Sport or Game

Write 8 sentences about your favourite sport or video game. You MUST use each of these correctly at least once: a, an, the, and – (no article).

After each sentence that uses an article, write in brackets WHY you used it. Like this:
"I play football every day." – (no article: sport / general)

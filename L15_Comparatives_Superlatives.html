Presentation

📌 Comparatives (comparing two things)

Adjective typeRuleExample
Short (1 syllable)+ -er + thanfast → faster than
Ends in -e+ -r + thanlarge → larger than
Short vowel + consonantdouble + -erbig → bigger than
Ends in consonant + y-y → -ierhappy → happier than
Long (2+ syllables)more + adjective + thanexpensive → more expensive than

📌 Superlatives (comparing three or more)

Adjective typeRuleExample
Shortthe + adjective + -estfast → the fastest
Longthe most + adjectiveexpensive → the most expensive
Irregulargood → better → the bestbad → worse → the worst
✅ A cheetah is faster than a lion.
✅ This game is more exciting than the last one.
✅ Everest is the highest mountain in the world.
✅ My little sister is funnier than me!
✅ This is the best day of my summer holidays.
💡 Irregular forms: good → better → the best. bad → worse → the worst. far → farther/further → the farthest/furthest.

📖 Amazing World Records

The world is full of incredible records. The blue whale is the biggest animal that has ever lived — it's even bigger than the biggest dinosaurs! The cheetah is the fastest land animal, but the peregrine falcon is faster than the cheetah when it dives from the sky. Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level, but the Mariana Trench is deeper than any ocean on Earth. The Sahara is one of the hottest and driest places, but Antarctica is colder and windier than almost anywhere else. Learning about these records is more exciting than reading normal facts, because everything is the biggest, the fastest, or the most extreme!

📖 Talent Show

Next Friday, Fatih's school is having a talent show. Fatih can play the recorder, so he wants to perform a song. His friend Ege can't sing very well, but he can do amazing skateboard tricks, so he's going to do that instead. Fatih's little sister can't join the show because she is too young — only students over eight can perform. The teacher says students can bring their own music, but they can't use very loud instruments in the small hall. Fatih is a little nervous because he can't remember all the notes yet, but his mum says he can practise every evening this week.

Write True, False, or Not Given.

Write 8 sentences about a talent show you would join. What can you do? What can't you do yet? Include a polite request and a permission question.

Think of your three favourite animals. Write 2 sentences about each one, saying what it can and can't do.

Presentation

📌 Subject Pronouns

PronounUsed forExample
IyourselfI am Fatih.
Youone person OR many peopleYou are my friend.
Hea man / boyHe is my brother.
Shea woman / girlShe is my teacher.
Itan animal or thingIt is a big dog.
Weyou + other peopleWe are from Turkey.
Theyother people or thingsThey are my friends.

📌 Have / Has

SubjectVerbExample
I / You / We / TheyhaveI have a dog.
He / She / IthasShe has a cat.
I / You / We / Theydon't haveI don't have a bike.
He / She / Itdoesn't haveHe doesn't have a phone.
✅ I have a dog. His name is Max.
✅ My friend has a PS5 — lucky him!
✅ We have football practice on Tuesday.
✅ She doesn't have a sister.
✅ He doesn't have a pet, but he wants one.
💡 Tip: Has is only for He / She / It. All other subjects use have. Easy rule: if you'd say "is", you also say "has"!

✍️ What Do You Have?

🗣️ Describe a Friend or Family Member

Talk for 1 minute about someone you know. Use subject pronouns and have/has.

  • Who is he/she? (name, age)
  • What does he/she have? (pet, game, phone…)
  • What doesn't he/she have?

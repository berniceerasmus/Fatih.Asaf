🔥 Can You Count It? (3 minutes)

Presentation

📌 Countable vs Uncountable

Countable (has plural)Uncountable (no plural)
an apple → appleswater, rice, milk, sugar
a book → booksbread, cheese, money
a dog → dogshomework, information, music

📌 Some / Any / How much / How many

UseRuleExample
Positive sentencessome (countable & uncountable)I have some apples. I have some water.
Negatives & questionsanyI don't have any apples. Do you have any water?
Questions – countableHow many + plural nounHow many apples do you have?
Questions – uncountableHow much + nounHow much water do you have?
Large quantity (both)a lot of / lots ofI have a lot of homework.
✅ I have some bananas in my bag.
✅ There isn't any milk in the fridge.
✅ How many books do you have? — I have five.
✅ How much water do you drink every day?
✅ We have a lot of homework tonight!
💡 Trick: If you can put a number in front of it (two apples, three dogs), it's countable. If not (two waters ❌), it's uncountable.

📖 Shopping List

Fatih's mum is writing a shopping list. She needs some apples, some bread, and a little cheese. She doesn't have any milk left, so she needs to buy some. There are some eggs in the fridge already, so she doesn't need more. She wants to buy two footballs for Fatih's birthday, but she isn't sure how much money she has. She also needs some rice for dinner, but she doesn't need any sugar because there is a lot of sugar in the cupboard. Fatih asks if he can have some chocolate, but his mum says there isn't any space left in the shopping bag!

Write True, False, or Not Given.

✍️ What's in Your Fridge?

Write 6 sentences about what is (and isn't) in your fridge or kitchen. Use some, any, how much and how many.

🗣️ Shopping Role Play

Imagine you are shopping with your teacher. Ask 5 questions using How much...? or How many...? and answer using some or any.

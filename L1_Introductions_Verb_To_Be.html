✅ My name is Fatih. I am 12 years old.
✅ My favourite team is Galatasaray. They are great!
✅ I am not from Ankara. I am from Eskişehir.
✅ Are you a gamer? — Yes, I am!
💡 Remember: In English we always use a subject pronoun. We can't say just "Is student." We must say "He is a student."

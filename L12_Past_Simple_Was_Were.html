📖 Last Weekend

Last weekend was very busy for Fatih and his family. On Saturday morning, they were at his grandma's house for breakfast. The food was delicious, but Fatih wasn't very hungry because he was excited about the football match later that day. In the afternoon, he was at the stadium with his dad — the match was amazing, and their team won! On Sunday, the weather was rainy, so they weren't outside much. Instead, Fatih and his sister were in the living room, playing board games. It wasn't a boring weekend at all — it was one of the best weekends of the year!

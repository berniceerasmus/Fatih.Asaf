📌 Negative & Questions (same as regular verbs!)

TypeStructureExample
NegativeSubject + didn't + base verbI didn't go to school.
QuestionDid + subject + base verb?Did you see the film?
✅ I went to my grandma's house yesterday.
✅ We had pizza for dinner last night.
✅ She didn't go to school on Monday — she was sick.
✅ Did you see the match? — Yes, I did!
💡 Remember: Even with irregular verbs, negatives and questions use didn't/did + base form.
❌ I didn't went.    ✅ I didn't go.

📖 A Trip to the Football Stadium

Last month, Fatih went to a professional football match with his dad. They took the metro into the city and got to the stadium two hours early. Fatih's dad bought him a team shirt from the shop outside, and they ate burgers before the game started. During the match, Fatih drank a fizzy drink and shouted for his team the whole time. His team scored two goals and won the match! After the game, Fatih wrote about the day in his diary. He didn't sleep well that night — he was too excited. It was the best day of his summer holidays.

Write True, False, or Not Given.

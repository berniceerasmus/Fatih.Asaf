📌 Regular Past Simple – Spelling Rules

RuleVerbPast Form
Most verbs: add -edplay, watch, washplayed, watched, washed
Ends in -e: add -dlike, dance, closeliked, danced, closed
Consonant + y → -iedstudy, cry, trystudied, cried, tried
Short vowel + consonant: double + -edstop, plan, chatstopped, planned, chatted

📌 Negative & Questions (all past simple verbs)

TypeStructureExample
NegativeSubject + didn't + base verbI didn't play yesterday.
QuestionDid + subject + base verb?Did you watch the film?
Short answerYes, I did. / No, I didn't.Did she study? — Yes, she did.
✅ I played football yesterday.
✅ She studied for her test last night.
✅ We didn't watch the match — it started too late.
✅ Did you talk to your teacher? — Yes, I did.
💡 Important: In negatives and questions, the main verb goes back to its base form — no -ed!
❌ I didn't played.    ✅ I didn't play.

📖 A Rainy Saturday

Last Saturday, it rained all day, so Fatih and his family stayed inside. In the morning, Fatih studied for his English test and finished all his homework. Then he played video games with his cousin online for an hour. His mum baked a chocolate cake in the afternoon, and everyone helped clean the kitchen afterwards. Fatih's dad fixed the broken shelf in the living room, and his sister painted a picture of their dog. They didn't watch TV in the evening — instead, they played a board game together and laughed a lot. Fatih didn't mind the rain at all; it was actually a really nice day at home.
